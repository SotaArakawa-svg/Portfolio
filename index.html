01 / About

About me

I'm an aspiring network and security engineer who learns by building. Rather than stopping at theory, I rack real firewalls, break things on purpose, and write up exactly what I did and why each step matters — the documents linked below are those write-ups.

My hands-on work spans two enterprise platforms. On Fortinet I've done initial SOHO bring-up, SSL VPN with FortiClient, and a managed wireless deployment with WPA2-Enterprise backed by a FreeRADIUS server. On Palo Alto I've recovered devices through maintenance mode, built zone-based SOHO networks with VLANs and DHCP, and stood up a GlobalProtect remote-access VPN with certificates.

Along the way I work comfortably with interfaces and DHCP, security zones and firewall policy, NAT, IPsec/SSL VPN, RADIUS authentication, and the CLI debugging that goes with all of it. I'm CCNA and CompTIA Security+ certified, and I'm looking for a role where I can keep going deeper.
